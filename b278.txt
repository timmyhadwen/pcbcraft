## What
The top tab bar becomes: Schematic | PCB Layout | Rules | Checks | Settings.
- Rules tab: the board rules and rule areas that live in the aside today.
- Checks tab: built-in findings plus the checks-you-write section, already the shape of rules_tab and the review groups.
- Settings tab: project settings and appearance, laid out in grouped sections with real insets (16 px side margins, spaced rows, wrapped helper text). The current settings wall is too big and has no margins.
- The left dock thins to library + tree only; nothing that belongs in a top tab stays in the aside.

## How
- crates/ui-egui/src/lib.rs: Tab enum at line 386 (add Rules, Settings), change_tab around line 830, the tab strip around line 2194; move rules_tab and the settings/inspector appearance block into tab bodies instead of the right aside.
