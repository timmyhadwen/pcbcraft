## What
Part of epic #322. Child of #324. Shares the stencil and cut-path geometry with #331 and the raster options with #328.

A laser job artifact for the owner's ComMarker B6 MOPA galvo fiber laser (JPT MOPA source, 1064 nm, 150 x 150 mm field, EZCAD2 board over USB), handed to the owner's laser program LaserCraft. Research note: `pcbcraft-research/shop-equipment.md` section 1 and profile `machine-profiles/commarker-b6-mopa.toml`.

The job is one SVG at true size in millimetres (explicit width, height and viewBox) with one group per operation, or a LaserCraft project JSON (`.lcproj`) with one cut setting per operation:
- `fiducials`: registration marks, marked first and used to align the second side.
- `isolation-cut`: closed polygons separating kept copper from copper to remove (Line).
- `copper-rubout`: filled regions of copper to ablate (Fill, line interval 0.025 mm, 45 degree hatch, 90 degree increment).
- `mask-open`: pad openings grown by the mask margin (Fill, low power).
- `legend`: silkscreen as filled polygons (Fill).
- `stencil-cut`: paste apertures with reduction plus frame and alignment holes (Line, kerf offset).
Each cut setting carries speed in mm/s, power percent, passes, frequency in kHz, pulse width in ns, and for fills the line interval and angles. The bottom side is mirrored about the chosen axis and carries the same fiducials. FR4 is not cut on this machine; drills and outline go to the mill.

## Why
The owner owns the B6 MOPA and wrote LaserCraft to drive it. The forums show copper ablation works on 20 to 60 W fiber sources but every job today is prepared by hand in the laser program; PCBCraft can hand over geometry already split by operation with the profile's starting parameters.

## How
- crates/doc: the `laser_galvo` profile kind (#324): field size, lens options, source type and wattage, frequency and pulse width ranges, max speed, and an operation table (name, mode line or fill, speed, power, frequency, pulse width, passes, line interval, hatch angle, angle increment, kerf offset, verified flag). Ship `commarker-b6-mopa` as a default profile with the starting values from the research note, all marked unverified.
- crates/io: `laser_job_svg(p, side, profile, options)` built on the existing `svg` writer (one `<g id>` per operation, mm units, closed paths only, no stroked centrelines) and `laser_job_lcproj(p, side, profile, options)` writing the LaserCraft project JSON: `{ app_version, shapes: [{ kind: "Path", cut_index, geometry: { Polyline: { points: [{x,y}], closed: true } } }], cut_settings: [{ index, name, speed_mm_s, power_min, power_max, num_passes, air_assist, galvo: { mode, frequency_khz, q_pulse_width_ns, global_passes, fill: { line_interval_mm, hatch_angle_deg, angle_increment_deg, ... }, line: { kerf_offset_mm, ... }, tabs, wobble, timing_override: null } }] }`. Isolation polygons come from the per-net copper union in crates/rules `copper` with a clearance offset; the stencil and cut paths come from #331; the mask openings reuse the soldermask growth from #325.
- crates/engine: artifact type `laser_job` with settings `{ side, operations: [..], format: svg|lcproj, mirror_axis, isolation_width_mm, rubout: bool, stencil_reduction_pct }` with bounds, and a field check (board plus margin must fit 150 x 150 mm, or 300 x 300 with the optional lens in the profile).
- Stays out of crates/ui-egui/src/lib.rs.

## Acceptance
- Golden fixture: the two-pad one-track board exported as SVG has width and height in mm equal to the board plus margin, six groups with the ids above (empty groups omitted), and only closed paths.
- Unit test: the `.lcproj` output parses as JSON with one cut setting per emitted group, each carrying frequency_khz and q_pulse_width_ns inside the profile ranges, and every shape's cut_index pointing at an existing setting.
- Unit test: the bottom side output is mirrored and its fiducials coincide with the top side's after the mirror.
- Unit test: a 160 x 160 mm board on the 150 mm field is refused with a plain reason and accepted when the profile lists the 300 mm lens.
- Owner decision (tag `owner-decision`): the source wattage (20, 30 or 60 W) and the lenses on hand; shop-equipment.md question 1.
