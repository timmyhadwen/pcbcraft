## What
A task-based user guide with step-by-step instructions for every area of the app, each step shown with a screenshot from the screenshot catalog. Source lives in the repo under docs/guide/ and is the content of the website's guide section.

## Goals
- Covers first run and getting started, projects and files, the schematic, the board, libraries and parts, rules and checks, the 3D view, outputs and fabrication, CAM and machines (laser, mill, pick and place, UV print), the assistant, settings, accessibility and languages, keyboard shortcuts.
- Plain language, short sentences, one task per page, written for a newcomer. Follows docs/doc-standard.md and the tooltip wording rules where they overlap.
- Each instruction step names the control exactly as the app labels it (from the text catalog), so a check can flag a guide step whose control label no longer exists.
- Every page has at least one screenshot from the catalog.

## Research questions
1. Structure: tutorials, how-to guides, reference and explanation as separate sections, or one task list. What well-regarded open source desktop app manuals do.
2. How to keep the guide from going stale: link steps to e2e stories, check control labels against en.txt, check screenshots exist.
3. Translation: can guide pages use the same languages as the app catalog, and how.
4. The full topic list, derived from the app's pages, menus and commands.
