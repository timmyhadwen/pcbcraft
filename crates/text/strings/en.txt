# Layers tree: what each row is (hover text on every row of both trees)
tree.explain.board = The board itself: its outline, its size and the stack of layers it is built from.
tree.explain.copper_layer = One layer of copper foil. The tracks, pads and pours on it carry the signals.
tree.explain.dielectric = The insulating core or prepreg between two copper layers. Its thickness sets the board's stackup.
tree.explain.copper = The copper foil on this side. Tracks, pads and pours that carry current live here.
tree.explain.mask = The coating over the copper. Openings in it expose pads for soldering.
tree.explain.silk = The ink printed on the board: part outlines, reference names and logos for whoever assembles it.
tree.explain.paste = The stencil layer for solder paste. Its openings say where paste goes on each pad.
tree.explain.components = The parts soldered on this side of the board.
tree.explain.parts = Every part placed on the board. Pick one to move it or edit its properties.
tree.explain.parts_sheet = Every part symbol in the design, from all pages.
tree.explain.drills = Drilled holes and vias. A via joins copper on different layers; a hole mounts or plugs in a part.
tree.explain.drill_size = The holes drilled with one bit size.
tree.explain.via = A plated hole that joins copper on different layers.
tree.explain.hole = A drilled hole with no copper connection, such as a mounting hole.
tree.explain.edge = The board edge: the outline the board is cut to, plus any cut-outs.
tree.explain.cutout = A round opening cut through the board.
tree.explain.user = Your own layers for notes, laser cuts or reference art that are not part of the copper stack.
tree.explain.art = A layer you drew yourself, such as a cut line or a reference outline.
tree.explain.shape = A line or shape drawn on this layer.
tree.explain.text = A text label placed on the board.
tree.explain.refs = The reference names, such as R1, printed next to each part.
tree.explain.outlines = The outline of each part printed on the board.
tree.explain.nets = Every named connection. A net joins the pins that must be wired together.
tree.explain.net = One named connection: the pins, tracks and wires that share a signal.
tree.explain.part = One part. Open it to see its pins or pads.
tree.explain.pad = The copper spot where a part's pin is soldered.
tree.explain.pin = One pin of a part and the net it connects to.
tree.explain.track = A strip of copper that carries a signal between two points.
tree.explain.zone = A filled copper area, often used for ground or power.
tree.explain.wire = A drawn connection between two pins.
tree.explain.label = A name tag that joins this point to the net with the same name.
tree.explain.page = One sheet of the schematic. Switch pages to draw a different part of the circuit.
tree.explain.symbols = The part symbols placed on this page.
tree.explain.power = Symbols that connect a wire to a power net such as GND or 3V3 without drawing the wire.
tree.explain.power_port = One power symbol and the net it ties to.
tree.explain.drawing = Plain lines, boxes and text you draw for notes. They do not connect electrically.
tree.explain.title = The box in the corner of the sheet with the title, date and revision.
